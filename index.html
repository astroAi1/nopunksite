About

The story

No-Punks is a simplified iteration of the CryptoPunks collection by colour palette reduction. The reduced palette and stripped detail push the characters toward abstraction while keeping the original silhouettes intact.

Every piece is built on a strict 24×24 grid, then upscaled for display. Traits stay; noise goes. It's all about seeing how little information you need before your brain still says "punk".

Launched on Base in 2024 and evolving under the broader NoMeta umbrella, the project keeps experimenting with derivatives, glitches, and new formats while staying loyal to the original pixel discipline.

Project Notes
  • 1:1 mapping to original CryptoPunks IDs
  • Traits preserved, palettes compressed
  • Strict black backgrounds, #040404 outlines
  • Built to last
